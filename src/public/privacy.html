ClipCatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

ClipCatch is an internal tool operated by CYOA Ltd (trading as CYOA Media, "we", "us"). It is used only by our own team to save Instagram Stories in which the artist and band accounts we manage have been tagged, before those Stories disappear after 24 hours. ClipCatch is not a public service, and no one outside CYOA Media has an account.

What we collect

When someone tags one of the Instagram accounts we manage in their Story, Instagram notifies ClipCatch. When a capture is running for that account, we save:

  • the photo or video from the Story that tagged the account;
  • the Instagram username of the person who posted it (and their Instagram ID, if the username cannot be looked up);
  • the date and time it was posted.

We also store the details needed to connect the accounts we manage: the account name, its Instagram ID and an access token that lets ClipCatch receive its Story mentions. We only connect accounts that we manage on behalf of our clients, with their permission.

We do not collect anything else about people who tag our accounts, we do not track anyone, and we do not use cookies for advertising or analytics.

Why we collect it

So our team can review tagged Stories and share or repost the ones we have permission to use, for example on the artist's own channels. We do not sell this content or data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties other than the service providers listed below.

Who can see it

Only CYOA Media team members who have a ClipCatch login. The content is not public.

Where it is stored

We use these providers to run ClipCatch:

  • Backblaze B2, for storing saved photos and videos;
  • Railway, for hosting the app and its records;
  • Meta (Instagram), which delivers the Story mentions to us.

How long we keep it

Saved content is kept until our team deletes it. When something is deleted it is held in a "Deleted Files" area for 30 days, in case it was removed by mistake, and then deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

If you tagged one of our accounts and want your Story content or username removed from ClipCatch, or want to know what we hold about you, email admin@cyoamedia.com. See our data deletion instructions.

Changes to this policy

If we change how ClipCatch handles data, we will update this page and the date above.
