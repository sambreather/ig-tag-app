ClipCatch Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 21 September 2026

ClipCatch is an internal tool run by CYOA Ltd (CYOA Media). It saves Instagram Stories in which the accounts we manage have been tagged. If you tagged one of those accounts and want your content or information removed, here is how.

To request deletion

  1. Email admin@cyoamedia.com with the subject "ClipCatch data deletion".
  2. Include your Instagram username, and the account you tagged if you remember it. This lets us find your content.
  3. We will delete the photos, videos and username we hold for you and reply to confirm, within 30 days of your request.

What gets deleted

  • Any photos or videos from your Stories that we saved;
  • your Instagram username and ID, and the timestamps linked to that content.

Deleted items are removed permanently. Nothing is kept in a backup once your request has been completed.

If you manage one of the accounts we connect

You can stop ClipCatch receiving your account's Story mentions at any time by removing the app in Instagram under Settings, then Website permissions (or Apps and websites). You can also email us to have your account's connection details deleted.

For more on how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.
